Our Story

A Table Set in the Spirit of the Poet

Ghalib Restaurant, a rooftop address on Tipu Sultan Road, invites patrons to indulge in an exquisite culinary journey through the flavors of South Asian, Mughlai and continental cuisine. With a rich heritage inspired by the legendary poet Mirza Ghalib, our establishment embodies elegance and tradition.

From aromatic biryanis to succulent BBQ kebabs, handi & karahi to brick-oven pizza, each dish is meticulously crafted to tantalize the senses. Dine under the open sky on our breezy rooftop, with attentive service and valet parking — or order online and let the journey come to you.
